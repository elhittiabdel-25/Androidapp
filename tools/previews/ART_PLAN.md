# Illustrated scenes: plan per card

Users asked for animations that show the idea or an example instead of moving text, with no growth in app size.
Every card gets an `art` scene (tools/cards/scenes.py, drawn in app/.../ui/Art.kt). One idea per card, taken
from the card's own text (its Quran/hadith imagery or the book's example). Objects, places and light only:
no people, animals, angels, the Throne, or depiction of Allah. Short tags in the picture, the caption below.

Status: key, rope, ship drawn (samples sent for approval). The rest waits for the user's go-ahead.
