Keeping the app light

  • Voice in Opus. Measured on today's 324 clips: 8.6 MB in Opus 24 kbps against 16.4 MB in MP3. New batches are voiced straight to Opus (Android plays it natively); today's clips stay as they are until then.
  • Recitations stay as published (64 kbps, about 0.15 MB per verse), because their licence allows redistribution only at the published bitrate. Verses shared between cards are stored once.
  • Google Play's size limits. The app's main download can be up to 500 MB (compressed). Above 200 MB, people on mobile data see a notice suggesting Wi-Fi, which they can skip. The plan passes 200 MB around v1.5 (seerah) and ends near 340 MB, so all 78 books fit in one download. Asset packs (Play Asset Delivery) stay in reserve: needed only above 500 MB, or to keep the first download under 200 MB.
  • Texts are small. Today's 46 cards take 242 KB; 700 cards come to about 4 MB.

  • The download workflow now finds each book's file on its library page. The 18 remaining Aqeedah books for v1.1 are downloaded (18 of 18, about 69 MB) and stored with the other sources.
  • Next session: write batch 3 (Aqeedah), voiced straight to Opus, with the same checks as before: text against the book, tashkeel, pronunciation, animation.
